Retail Inventory Optimization & Demand Forecasting

Project Overview

This project analyzes 76,000 simulated retail inventory records across 5 stores and 20 products to explore inventory distribution, sales performance, and demand forecasting.

Using Microsoft Excel, I developed an analytical dashboard to identify inventory-to-demand variations, evaluate forecasting models, and support data-driven inventory planning recommendations.

Key Findings

Total Units Sold: 6,750,876

Total Demand: 7,928,104

Sales-to-Demand Ratio: 85.15%

3-Day Moving Average MAPE: 7.40%

7-Day Moving Average MAPE: 8.08%

Inventory-to-demand ratios varied across products and stores, highlighting opportunities for further investigation into inventory allocation and replenishment planning.

The 3-day moving average produced a lower reported MAPE than the 7-day model, although their evaluation periods differ slightly.

Business Recommendations

Investigate product-store combinations with unusually high or low inventory-to-demand ratios.

Use short-term demand forecasting to support inventory replenishment decisions.

Monitor forecast accuracy and adjust forecasting methods as demand patterns change.

Dataset

This project uses a synthetic retail inventory dataset sourced from Kaggle. Results are illustrative and do not represent actual company operations.
